Nuadyr-mpv is a video and audio player for Android TV, phones and tablets. It is a fork of mpv-android (https://github.com/mpv-android/mpv-android) and plays with libmpv (https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv).

What's different:
* Library home screen: a thumbnail grid of all your videos and audio on TV, phone and tablet
* Sort, filter and group the library; folder cards for folders with several files
* Resume where you stopped, with progress bars on the tiles
* Auto queue: the files after the one you pick play next
* Frame rate matching on Android TV (Android 11 or newer)
* Aengus theme, light and dark

From mpv-android:
* Hardware and software video decoding
* Gesture-based seeking, volume/brightness control and more
* libass support for styled subtitles, secondary (or dual) subtitles
* Advanced video settings (interpolation, debanding, scalers, ...)
* Play network streams with the "Open URL" function
* Background playback, Picture-in-Picture, keyboard input supported
